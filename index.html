10 · Market problems
1 · Corporations / project developers
Lack a transparent, verifiable and scalable underlying asset to support their financing needs.
They need predictable, even “programmable”, demand for the goods and services they finance, yet rely on market forecasts and on ESG data that rating and data providers gather mainly from companies’ own public disclosures and questionnaires — or estimate when such data are missing.1
<1 bp
pricing advantage for green-bond issuers worldwide, 20252
2 · Asset Managers
Lack a sufficient supply of transparent and verified Sustainable & ESG products for mandates and portfolios.
Due diligence still rests on external ESG ratings and data built largely from companies’ own disclosures — and the ratings diverge widely. In Europe, only 13 new sustainable funds were launched in Q2 2026, against 64 closures.4
0.54
average correlation between the ESG ratings of major agencies, vs 0.92 for Moody’s and S&P credit ratings3
3 · Non-bank financial institutions (NBFIs) and sovereign funds
Face difficulty verifying that Sustainable & ESG capital produces measurable real-world outcomes.
Pension funds, insurers and other NBFIs, as well as sovereign wealth funds, are exposed to reputational and regulatory risk from greenwashing5. They need a clear, data-driven link between capital deployed and verified outcomes.
44%
of investors agree that corporate sustainability reporting contains unsupported claims “to a large or very large extent”6

Asset Managers structure the Data Asset Package to attract capital
They integrate it into financial structures for NBFI capital, subject to legal qualification in each jurisdiction.
→ Asset Managers → instruments → NBFI capital
07 · REINVESTMENT LOOP
Computing capacity generates additional impact
Runs superintelligence services and Sustainable City and SDG programs; creates new Data Asset Packages.

Potentiacta receives CASH and uses COMPUTE CAPACITY
① CASH

② COMPUTE CAPACITY

Corporations expand AI supercomputing infrastructure
The capital raised goes into new AI infrastructure capacity, including GPUs and other high-performance computing.
→ Sustainable & ESG capital → AI infrastructure
For corporations

For Potentiacta
Revenue first keeps every basket prepaid (Data Asset sales ≥ basket volume); the rest, plus the rounds, goes into supercomputing infrastructure for superintelligence.
Bottom line
Sell → receive → compute → generate → sell again. Never a guaranteed return; valuation needs an independent advisor.

17 · Competitive landscape
Crowded in reporting and ratings — thin at the asset layer.

More than 100 ESG rating providers — including units of MSCI, Morningstar Sustainalytics, S&P Global, ISS and Bloomberg — have notified ESMA that they intend to keep operating in the EU under the new ESG Ratings Regulation.1 Their products serve reporting, disclosure, scoring and compliance, and their data already feed indexes behind investment products.2

Among the providers we reviewed (public pages, 6 Oct 2026), none sells a verified, transaction-level impact data asset designed for institutional investors. The closest precedents are project-based: Verra's SD VISta issues standardized, transactable units for independently verified sustainable-development benefits, and its VCS program issues tradable carbon credits.3

Where we aim
The space between impact measurement and capital formation: transaction-level data → quantified [total ΔY] → independently verified → packaged → valued → designed for use by Asset Managers in structured financing, subject to legal qualification.
Incumbents measure and report ESG. Potentiacta is built to turn measured impact into Data Asset Packages.
